# Design analysis

No reference images were provided (`docs/design/references/` is empty), so this follows the default direction from `docs/steps/step-2-design-and-mascot.md`. Principles only; the character is original.

- **Shape language:** round and soft. One dominant ellipse body, small rounded details, no sharp corners except the beak.
- **Proportions:** big head-body (one shape), tiny feet, a small leaf as the only "antenna". Readable at 96 px.
- **Palette mood:** fresh lime body, cream belly with dark seeds, warm orange beak and feet, blue as the single "system" accent (listening ring, marks). Flat colors, no gradients or glow.
- **Face:** two big dark eyes with one highlight each, a small beak, light blush. Expression changes by eye shape (closed, half, happy arcs, crossed), not by color.
- **Motion timing:** slow breathing (3 s), quick blink (160 ms), occasional glance and hop, 0.5 s hop with slight overshoot. All loops stop under reduced motion.
- **Panel layout:** header (name, state, mic dot), scrolling conversation, composer at the bottom. Receipts are compact one-liners with a status chip so actions read as a tape.
- **Avoid:** gloss, glow, heavy shadows, copying any existing character, color-only status, long speech bubbles.
