# Product: Kiwi

Kiwi is a downloadable desktop assistant. A cute animated mascot lives in a corner of the screen and a small chat panel docks directly below it. The user types, or says "Hey Kiwi", and Kiwi acts on the computer: opens apps and links, searches, researches, sets timers and notes, controls volume, describes the screen, sorts files in a sandbox folder, and can delegate coding tasks.

## Users

People who want a friendly, trustworthy helper on their own machine without sending their data to a cloud service.

## Principles

- Every action is visible (receipt lines in the chat) and undoable where possible.
- Local first: reasoning runs on open-weight models through Ollama; the core works offline.
- Safe by default: allowlisted apps, sandboxed files, confirmation before changes.
- Warm and small: the mascot is an original character, never a copy of an existing one.

## Tone

Short, kind, plain sentences. Never pretends to have done something it has not.

## Non-goals

Sending messages, emails or purchases; arbitrary shell execution; cloud-only features.
